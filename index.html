1. HertsActive — Collaborative WordPress Website Development

HertsActive was a collaborative website project focused on health, wellbeing and encouraging people to explore healthier lifestyles. The website covered activities and interests such as gym workouts, yoga, outdoor activities and park running.

I worked in a three-person team alongside Sazid and Alanzo. My responsibilities included project coordination, contributing to the website's development, creating two content pages and adding a creative writing section. I also implemented the website's cookie functionality and contributed to identifying suitable solutions to technical challenges during the development process.

Key Contributions:

  • Coordinated project activities and supported task organisation across the three-person team.
  • Developed two dedicated website content pages focused on campus health and fitness activities.
  • Created and integrated a creative writing section to boost user engagement.
  • Implemented website cookie functionality for user session awareness and compliance.
  • Helped identify practical solutions to coding and layout challenges during deployment.
  • Collaborated closely with team members to deliver the project within the available timeframe.

Skills Developed: WordPress, website development, project coordination, teamwork, problem-solving, and time management.
