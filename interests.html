Technical Interests

Exploring how modern technology, business strategy, and organisational innovation intersect to solve real-world challenges.

Enterprise Systems and Digital Transformation

I am interested in how organisations use information systems and digital technologies to improve business processes, manage resources and increase operational efficiency. Modern enterprises depend on integrated digital workflows to eliminate bottlenecks and unify communication across departments.

I want to understand how scalable technology architectures can support long-term organisational growth, streamline legacy operations, and empower strategic decision-making.

Financial Technology (FinTech)

I am interested in the transformation of financial services through software, digital platforms, automation and emerging technologies. Digital banking, automated payment infrastructures, and accessible financial tools are reshaping how individuals and businesses interact with capital.

I particularly enjoy exploring the relationship between secure software engineering, regulatory compliance, financial services and modern business models.

Business Intelligence and Data Analytics

I am interested in how businesses collect, analyse and interpret data to make better, evidence-based decisions. Turning raw operational metrics into clear visual insights allows organisations to spot market trends early and optimise performance.

I want to develop my understanding of business intelligence tools, relational reporting systems, and data-driven strategies during my placement year.

Project Management and Technology Consulting

I enjoy the organisational side of technology projects, including planning, coordinating people, managing resources and working towards deadlines. Even strong technical architectures require structured coordination and clear stakeholder communication to succeed.

I am interested in how technical knowledge can be translated into practical business solutions that align directly with client goals.

Digital Marketing

I am also interested in the use of digital platforms, technology and data to understand customers, develop marketing strategies and support sustainable business growth. Combining web usability with analytics allows organisations to engage their audiences more meaningfully.
